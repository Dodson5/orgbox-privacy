OrgBox — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-13

OrgBox is a Chrome extension that lets you stay signed in to multiple Xero organisations at the same time by keeping each session's cookies isolated in its own private container.

The short version

OrgBox does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything it stores stays on your own computer, inside your Chrome profile. There are no OrgBox servers, no analytics, and no third parties.

What OrgBox stores, and why

All of the following is stored locally using Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage) and never leaves your device:

  • Session cookies for Xero sites (*.xero.com). These are the cookies that keep you logged in. OrgBox keeps a separate, private copy per session so your organisations stay isolated. They are stored in the same place, and with the same protection, as Chrome's own cookie storage.
  • Session details you can see in the extension: each session's name, colour, and the last Xero page it was on (so "Open" can take you back there). Auth pages are never stored as a return location.
  • Tab and group associations (which open tab belongs to which session).
  • A local diagnostics log of recent extension events (e.g. "session created", "logged in"), capped to the most recent few hundred entries, used only for troubleshooting. It records page origins and paths but never query strings, cookie values, passwords, or page contents. You can clear it at any time from the extension's options page.

What OrgBox reads

  • The page title of the Xero tab you are viewing, so a session can rename itself to the organisation you are in. Only the visible tab is read, and only the title text is used.

What OrgBox does NOT do

  • It does not send any data to any server — there is no backend.
  • It does not include analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • It does not read or store data from sites other than Xero (*.xero.com).
  • It does not record passwords, MFA codes, page contents, or cookie values in its diagnostics log.

Permissions, in plain terms

  • cookies / host access to *.xero.com — to keep each session's Xero login isolated in its own container.
  • declarativeNetRequest / webRequest — to attach the correct session's cookies to each tab's requests, locally, as they happen.
  • tabs / tabGroups — to colour-group a session's tabs and know which tab belongs to which session.
  • storage — to remember your sessions between browser restarts, on your device only.
  • notifications — to alert you if something goes wrong.

Your control over your data

  • Deleting a session in OrgBox erases that session's stored cookies and details.
  • The "Clean Xero cookies from real jar" action clears Xero cookies from Chrome's normal cookie store.
  • Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything OrgBox has stored.
